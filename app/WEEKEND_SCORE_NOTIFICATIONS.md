# Weekend score notifications

Result fetching and notification delivery are separate background checks:

- Results: calendar-worker checks at startup and hourly, with sessions eligible three hours after GP start or one hour after Sprint start. A maximum of three sessions is processed per pass; older pending sessions rotate. Saved organiser drafts, unresolved replacements and uncertain source data remain reviewable. Restarting an updated worker triggers its initial pass. Manual Fetch does not publish.
- Notifications: the existing push worker checks every five minutes. Delivery can therefore take up to roughly five minutes after full publication, plus browser/device delivery time or queue backlog. The initial startup result check finishes before the worker starts its five-minute push timer.

Players enable Push notifications in Profile on each device. iOS requires the installed Home Screen app. Browser and OS permission, connectivity and notification settings still control delivery. Notifications show the league and weekend name and invite the player to view updated standings; they do not expose predictions or individual scores on the lock screen. Tapping opens the app. English and Dutch text follow the subscription language.

`weekend-scores.ts` detects a transition to fully published GP and, when applicable, Sprint. Both the automatic scheduler and manual publication record `Event.scoresPublishedAt` in their publication transaction. Draft saves, repeat publication and corrections do not change this marker. Existing historical results without the marker are not backfilled. No new notification is queued for practice events.

`push.ts` combines score jobs with the existing deadline reminders, prioritising up to 20 score jobs per pass and sending in batches of four. Eligibility requires a real active season, active player season membership, a current opted-in device registered no later than publication, a fully published weekend, and a publication no more than 48 hours old. These conditions are rechecked before sending. Stable per-device/event/publication keys, leases and delivery records prevent normal duplicate runs; failed sends retry with the existing five-attempt cap and five-minute backoff. Provider 404/410 removes the expired subscription. Browser notification tags also use the stable key. As with ordinary Web Push, a process failure after external delivery but before recording success can cause a retry.

All external delivery occurs outside state transactions. No notifications are sent from a browser click or result publication transaction. No new secret, API key, email provider or database migration is required; update the app and calendar-worker together when installing the release.

The results panel uses a scoped grid layout so it does not change other info bars. It shows the last provider attempt time, not a promise that the current message was generated by a newly installed version. A published result takes precedence over an old saved waiting reason. The deployed worker logs and installed version are needed to diagnose a worker that is not making new attempts.
